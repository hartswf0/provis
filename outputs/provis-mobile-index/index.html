PROVISelectronic medical records for ventures

Problem-Oriented Venture Information System

PROVIS converts public-source venture traces into auditable case files for startups, founders, claims, relationships, risks, contradictions, and unresolved evidence gaps.

Diagnose the record. Do not trust the pitch.

OriginPROMIS / POMR lineage
MedicinePROMIS forced clinical data, notes, diagnoses, and interventions to attach to medical problems.
Venture IntelligencePROVIS forces sources, claims, founder links, risks, contradictions, and probes to attach to venture problems.
Core QuestionNot “is this a promising startup?” but “what does the evidence actually support?”
OutputHere is the case. Here are the problems. Here is what is proven, weak, missing, contradicted, and worth probing next.

How It Worksoperator workflow
1 / CaptureRegister a public destination source. Search pages are discovery only, not final evidence.
2 / FormConvert loose information into typed objects: Evidence Form, Claim Form, Relationship Edge, Problem Form, Trace.
3 / BindAttach each claim or edge to evidence, with both supports and does-not-establish visible.
4 / DiagnoseMake gaps and contradictions first-class problems instead of hiding them in notes.
5 / ProbeTurn uncertainty into the next public-source hunt.
6 / ExportRun Quickcheck, preserve trace, and compile the venture case file.

Rulesanti-mythology
Evidence Before NarrativeNo claim without a source. No edge without evidence.
Problems FirstMissing founder proof, stale accelerator pages, unsupported traction, and contradictions are first-class objects.
Uncertainty PreservedPROVIS does not pretend to know what it cannot know. It names uncertainty and turns it into a probe.
Public BoundaryProfessional risk is allowed. Private dirt is not. No private emails, phones, home addresses, protected attributes, leaks, or login-gated material.
ToneForensic, operational, skeptical, bounded, traceable, direct, calmly adversarial.
PositioningNot Crunchbase, PitchBook, LinkedIn, CRM, lead scraper, surveillance tool, pitch summarizer, or generic AI research assistant.
